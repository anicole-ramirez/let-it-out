On your phone

Two buttons. That’s the whole controller.

LET IT OUT
Brandt is speaking. Boo him!

Clap while your candidate speaks, boo while the other one does. Every tap sends something flying onto the stage above: flowers and teddies for your side, tomatoes and old shoes for theirs.

  • Big round buttons you can hit without looking.
  • The ring around each button is your team’s colour.
  • Try it: the buttons on this phone throw into the stage at the top.

The cast

Bobbleheads in team colours

One character drawn in six body types, ten hats, six hairstyles, noses, beards and extras. Suit and hat always carry the team colour. The referee is the only one in black and white.
